BILAFORM INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Information you provide

If you create an account, BilaForm stores your name, email address, a bcrypt password hash, and account creation time in its SQLite database. The original password is not stored. If you choose to save a plan, the latest cash, essentials, buffer, days to payday and calculated daily amount are associated with your account. Contact form submissions store your name, email address, message and submission time.

How information is used and stored

Account information is used to sign you in and retrieve a saved plan. Contact details and messages are stored so the BilaForm team can follow up; this version does not automatically email or forward submissions. The database is stored on the server running BilaForm. The site stores your signed JSON Web Token and display name in browser local storage until you log out or the token expires after seven days. Do not use a shared device if you do not want the account to remain signed in.

The runway calculator works in your browser and can still calculate when the API is offline. When connected, the entered amounts are also sent to BilaForm’s API for server-side validation and calculation. The service worker may cache website pages and files on your device for offline access. The page requests fonts from Google Fonts, which receives normal connection information needed to deliver those files.

Retention and security

This version does not yet include self-service account deletion, contact-message deletion, or automatic retention periods. The site uses HTTPS when deployed securely and hashes account passwords, but no online service can promise absolute security. Operators should restrict access to the server and database, protect backups, establish retention and deletion procedures, and configure a strong JWT secret.

Your choices and rights

You can log out to remove the stored sign-in token from this browser. For requests about account or contact information, use the contact form and identify the email address associated with your request. Applicable privacy rights depend on the circumstances and governing law. Review the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner guidance.

Contact and review

Use the BilaForm contact form for privacy questions. The site stores submissions for follow-up but does not forward them automatically. This notice should be reviewed against the actual production hosting, database access, retention and support arrangements before public launch.
