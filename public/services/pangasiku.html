PangaSiku: Your daily budget guide.

PangaSiku estimates a daily safe-to-spend amount from the money available after upcoming essentials and a buffer are reserved. The remaining amount is divided by the days until your next income.

Open the PangaSiku planner

How this tool helps

PangaSiku estimates a daily safe-to-spend amount from the money available after upcoming essentials and a buffer are reserved. The remaining amount is divided by the days until your next income.

Designed for your own numbers

Enter realistic figures in Kenyan Shillings (KES). The result is a planning estimate; it does not connect to a bank or replace professional financial advice.
