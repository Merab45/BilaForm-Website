A USEFUL PLACE TO START

A daily guide built from your actual numbers.

PangaSiku begins with the money you can use, adds any income you expect before payday, then accounts for essential expenses and a buffer you choose. The remaining amount is divided by the days in your plan. If your commitments exceed the money entered, the guide shows the shortfall and sets daily spending to KES 0 until you review it.

You can try the calculator without an account. Sign in when you want to save and update a plan. Estimates depend on the information you enter; BilaForm does not know about costs you leave out.

BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

What is BilaForm?

BilaForm is a set of personal money planning tools. It helps you organise costs, explore a daily spending estimate and think through choices using the amounts you provide.

Do I need an account to use a tool?

You can use the PangaSiku calculator as a guest. An account is needed to save a plan and access account features such as plan history and spending entries.

Does BilaForm connect to my bank or mobile wallet?

No. Enter the amounts you want to plan with yourself. BilaForm does not import balances or transactions from your financial accounts.

Is the daily amount guaranteed to be safe?

No. It is an estimate based on the figures and dates you enter. Unexpected or unlisted expenses can change what you can spend, so review your plan as needed.

How can I get help?

Use the contact form to send the BilaForm team a message. It is stored for follow-up and is not emailed automatically, so include an email address where you can be reached.
