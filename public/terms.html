Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Personal finance planning

BilaForm provides budgeting and planning tools. It is not a bank, payment service, financial institution, lender, or a substitute for professional financial advice. It does not connect to bank or mobile money accounts or move funds.

Calculator and examples

The calculator divides the amount remaining after the essentials and buffer you enter by the days until payday. Its result is an estimate based only on those inputs. Dashboard figures and illustrations are samples, not live account balances, quotes, or promises of an outcome.

Accounts and saved plans

You are responsible for keeping your sign-in credentials private and for logging out on shared devices. Saved budgets are planning inputs associated with your account, not verified bank balances or financial records. Contact messages can be submitted through the website; this version stores them but does not automatically send an email reply.

Availability

We work to keep BilaForm available but cannot guarantee uninterrupted access, data recovery, or suitability for every purpose. Keep your own records for important financial decisions. Calculations and examples are estimates based on the information entered and can differ from actual expenses.
