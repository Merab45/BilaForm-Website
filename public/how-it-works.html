COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know

What is BilaForm?

BilaForm is a KES-focused personal finance platform with tools to help plan spending between income days.

How is the daily guide calculated?

Subtract essentials and your chosen buffer from available money, then divide the remaining amount by the days to payday.

Does BilaForm connect to my bank?

No. BilaForm does not connect to bank accounts or move money. You enter the planning amounts yourself.

Can I save my plan?

Yes. Sign in, use Save Plan in PangaSiku, and the saved calculation will appear in your client portal.
