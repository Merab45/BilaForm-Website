GET STARTED

A clearer money plan, step by step.

Use your own figures to plan the days until your next income.

Open PangaSiku
  1. 01

    Set your planning window

    Enter the money you can use, where it is held, your next payday and any other income you reasonably expect before then.

  2. 02

    List commitments due

    Add expenses such as food, transport, rent or repayments only when they are due before payday. The list adds up automatically.

  3. 03

    Choose a buffer and review

    Set aside an amount that suits you. PangaSiku shows the resulting balance, days in the plan and a daily estimate. If listed costs exceed available money, it shows the shortfall and a zero daily guide.

  4. 04

    Save and check in

    Try the calculation as a guest, or sign in to save and update a plan. Signed-in customers can log daily spending and compare it with the saved guide.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know

What is BilaForm?

BilaForm is a KES-focused personal finance platform with tools to help plan spending between income days.

How is the daily guide calculated?

Subtract essentials and your chosen buffer from available money, then divide the remaining amount by the days to payday.

Does BilaForm connect to my bank?

No. BilaForm does not connect to bank accounts or move money. You enter the planning amounts yourself.

Can I save my plan?

Yes. Sign in, use Save Plan in PangaSiku, and the saved calculation will appear in your client portal.
