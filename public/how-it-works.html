GET STARTED

A clearer money plan, step by step.

Use your own figures to plan the days until your next income.

  1. 01

    Enter your numbers

    Add available cash, essentials, a buffer and days to your next income.

  2. 02

    Review the daily guide

    PangaSiku estimates what remains after the amounts you set aside, divided across your chosen days.

  3. 03

    Compare a purchase

    Use the purchase simulator to consider how an item could change your daily guide.

  4. 04

    Save your plan

    Sign in to save a PangaSiku plan to your account and revisit it later.
